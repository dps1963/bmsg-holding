About the group

What is BringMeSunshine Group?
BringMeSunshine Group is an independent maker of warm, human technology that helps people do good, learn well and live better. One group, three products, one purpose: goodness, education and wellbeing for all.
What products do you make?
Three: OneGoodDeed (a live map of good deeds around the world), Our Family Rocks (a calm family-organising app), and Learn to Read · Read to Learn (an AI reading companion for any age). Each carries the same promise into a different corner of life.
What is your mission?
We believe small, everyday acts — a kind deed, a shared family moment, a page read aloud — compound into brighter lives, so we build products that make those acts easier for individuals and the groups around them.

Using the products

Which product is right for me?
Want to log a kind act and see kindness spread? OneGoodDeed. Want to bring a busy household together? Our Family Rocks. Want to help someone learn to read, at any age? Learn to Read · Read to Learn. You can use any or all of them.
Do the products share one account?
No. Each product has its own account and sign-in, so your family app and your reading app stay separate. They are sibling products under one group, not one merged app.
Are the products free?
Our Family Rocks is free to download and Learn to Read is free to start; OneGoodDeed is free to use with optional donations that help keep the platform running. See each product's own FAQ for details.

Privacy & contact

How do you handle my data and privacy?
Your data is stored on EU-resident infrastructure and handled under GDPR. We use privacy-respecting, self-hosted analytics — never Google Analytics or a Meta Pixel, anywhere. Each product has its own privacy policy with the specifics.
Do you use my data to train AI?
We do not sell your data or use your personal content to train external AI models. Our reading app adapts to a learner on their own account, and our support assistants answer from published help content — not from other users' private data.
How do I get in touch?
Use the contact link on any of our sites or email support@ for the product you're using. A person will get back to you; our AI support assistant can also help with common questions right away.
Where are you based and where is my data stored?
All persistent data lives on EU-resident infrastructure, which is lawful for our users in New Zealand and elsewhere under the relevant adequacy arrangements. We chose EU residency deliberately for strong privacy protection.
